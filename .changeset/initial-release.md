First public release. `@kurenwimpel/core` evaluates flags with targeting, segments, prerequisites, traffic allocation and deterministic rollouts. `@kurenwimpel/node` and `@kurenwimpel/cloudflare` load rulesets on Node.js and Cloudflare Workers, and `@kurenwimpel/ofrep` describes the OFREP wire contract as Standard Schema definitions.
